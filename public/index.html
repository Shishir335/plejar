What you can do in Plezar

Share trips in a travel feed

Post photos, videos and short notes from wherever you are. Every post is tagged with a place, so the feed doubles as a living map of Bangladesh: what Ratargul looks like this week, how high the water is at the haor, whether the Sajek road is open.

  • Follow travelers and agents whose trips you like
  • Like, comment and save posts for later
  • Browse by place, season or type of trip

Check the agent before you call

Every tour agent gets a profile with the trips they have run, photos from those trips and reviews from travelers who went. Agents who show a trade license and national ID get a verified mark.

  • See an agent's past trips, not just their ads
  • Read reviews from people who travelled with them
  • Message the agent directly from their profile

Guides for every place

Each destination has its own page, built from what travelers post: the best months to go, how to get there from Dhaka, what to pack, and the latest photos. Nafakhum in the dry season is a different trip from Nafakhum in the monsoon, and the page shows both.

  • Best season and current conditions
  • Routes and travel time from Dhaka
  • Agents who run trips there

Plan trips with friends

Save places and posts into a trip plan, arrange them day by day, and share the plan with the people you are going with. Everyone can add ideas, so the group chat doesn't have to hold the whole plan.

  • Collect saved places into one plan
  • Organise it by day
  • Share and edit it together

For travelers

Plezar is free for travelers. Use it to find where to go next, and to see what a trip is really like before you leave Dhaka.

  • Find ideas from real tripsSee where people actually went last weekend, not a five-year-old blog post.
  • Know what to expectPhotos and notes show the road, the rooms and the weather before you leave.
  • Choose agents with confidenceCompare their past trips and reviews side by side.
  • Keep your travel storyYour posts become a map of everywhere you have been in Bangladesh.

For tour agents

We are inviting a first group of tour agents to set up their profiles before Plezar opens to everyone. Tell us about your agency and our team will get in touch.

  • Show your work. Every trip you post adds to a portfolio people can browse any time.
  • Grow followers who care. People who follow you see your new trips first.
  • Earn trust with reviews. Honest reviews from real travelers stay on your profile.
  • Get verified. Show your trade license once and stand out from unknown pages.
